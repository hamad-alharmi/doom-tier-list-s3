THE RULEBOOK

Keep it clean.

Fight rules

01
Max 25ms gap

Both opponents must have a ping difference of no higher than 25 ms. Bigger gap — the fight doesn't count.

02
No fighting AFK

If someone is AFK, they're not ready to fight. Do not fight them unless they move.

03
Zero cheats

No cheating, no illegal mods or cheat clients. Get caught and you're banned off the server.

Mod rules

ALLOWED
  • Cosmetic mods with no unreasonable advantage — capes, crosshairs, block overlays, etc.
  • Performance mods that don't impact gameplay itself.
  • HUD mods about you, the player — armor HUD, potion HUD, saturation HUD, etc.
DISALLOWED — INSTANT BAN
  • Mods that modify movement, reach, or circumvent typical PvP interactions.
  • Automation of PvP — macros, auto-clickers, auto totem, etc.
  • Substantial irregularities vs a vanilla client — moving while in inventory, block-placement changes.
  • Visibility/perception advantages — ESP, radar, free-look.
  • Tracking opponent info — armor durability, health, saturation.
  • Double-binding controls (except mice that require it for button binding).
  • Mods that unreasonably hurt your connection or latency.
QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Both players ban one kit and pick the kit they want, then the leftover kits get randomly rolled. You fight first to 3 until the final fight, which is first to 5. The winner advances to the next round while the loser stays behind — the whole tournament is bracket style and lives on this site.

You fight the ranked player in all kits, and your rank depends on how you performed against them. A close 3–2 can still earn a shot at B tier if you performed well enough.

The kickoff tournament seeds the first rankings, cross-checked against verified lists like MCTiers. After that, call your shot: fight a C-Tier player while Valu watches, and Valu makes the final call.

Ping difference can't be higher than 25 ms, AFK players aren't ready to fight, and cheating of any kind gets you banned off the server. Full rulebook is in the section above.
